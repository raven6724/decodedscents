#5Maison Alhambra Maitre de Blue

86% match  ·  $25  ·  Woody Aromatic
Top Grapefruit, Lemon, Mint, Pink Pepper
Heart Ginger, Nutmeg, Jasmine, Iso E Super
Base Incense, Vetiver, Cedar, Sandalwood, Patchouli, Labdanum, White Musk

Shares with the original: Grapefruit, Lemon, Mint, Pink Pepper, Ginger, Nutmeg, Jasmine, Incense, Cedar, Sandalwood, Patchouli, Labdanum

Twelve shared molecules with Bleu de Chanel EDP -- the highest note-level overlap of any BdC alternative we have researched. A Fragrantica reviewer running an explicit side-by-side against the EDP found it "very, very similar," noting BdC is a little smoother and sweeter in both opening and drydown but that without a direct A/B comparison, out in the wild they doubt they could tell them apart. A FragranceNet buyer rates the likeness 9.5/10, and one reviewer puts it at roughly 90% of BdC EDP after several months of maceration. Read this one honestly before buying: the target is disputed. Several independent reviewers insist it matches the EDT rather than the EDP, one calls it a Parfum clone, the missing coriander is reported to cost it some of BdC's warmth and smoothness, the woody and smoky notes run weaker than the original, and longevity reports scatter widely from about an hour to a full day. If you want the EDP specifically, Club de Nuit Iconic is the safer pick; if you want the Bleu profile cheaply and are relaxed about which concentration it lands nearest, this is the best value in the set.

Sources: Fragrantica explicit EDP side-by-side review, FragranceNet buyer likeness rating, Scento-quoted 90% maceration comparison, Melissa Jane Lee Maison Alhambra dupe list
