What Is a Fragrance Dupe?

A fragrance dupe — also known as an affordable perfume clone or luxury fragrance alternative — is a perfume that smells nearly identical to a high-end designer or niche fragrance, but costs a fraction of the price. At Decoded Scents, we define a true dupe as any fragrance that scores 85% or higher in community similarity testing against its luxury counterpart. Anything below that threshold is simply "inspired by" — and we don't include those in our results.

In our testing, we cross-reference thousands of fragrance community reviews from Reddit r/fragrance, Fragrantica, Basenotes, Parfumo, and verified purchase reviews on Amazon and Walmart. We noticed that the most reliable similarity scores come from reviewers who own both the original and the alternative — not just people who have smelled one in a store. That is the standard we hold every entry in our database to.

Our database currently covers luxury perfume dupes for fragrances from Creed, Tom Ford, Maison Francis Kurkdjian, Kilian Paris, Byredo, Amouage, Parfums de Marly, Penhaligon's, Maison Margiela, and more. In our testing, brands like Lattafa, Maison Alhambra, Armaf, Dossier, and Afnan consistently produce the most accurate affordable perfume clones — often achieving 88-95% similarity to originals that cost 10 times more.

Every result you see on Decoded Scents is backed by real community research. We noticed that generic "inspired by" lists published across the web are filled with invented product names and unverified claims. Our rule is simple: if we cannot find verified community confirmation of the similarity, it does not appear in our results. One honest result is worth more than ten invented ones.
