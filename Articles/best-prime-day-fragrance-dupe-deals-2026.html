Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Prime Day fragrance dupe shopping.

When is Amazon Prime Day 2026?

Amazon Prime Day 2026 runs from June 23 through June 26 — a four-day sale event with deals refreshed daily. Fragrance dupes typically see their deepest discounts in the first 48 hours.

Do fragrance dupes really go on sale during Prime Day?

Yes. Brands like Lattafa, Armaf, Afnan, Maison Alhambra, Al Haramain, and Dossier have all run meaningful Prime Day discounts in past years, with savings typically between 15% and 40% off their already-low everyday Amazon prices. Stock often sells out fast on the most popular dupes — Bade'e Al Oud and Club de Nuit Intense Man, in particular, tend to go quickly.

What is a fragrance dupe?

A fragrance dupe is a perfume that closely mimics the scent profile of a more expensive designer or niche fragrance, usually at 5-15% of the original's price. Quality dupes share similar top, heart, and base notes and often perform comparably in longevity and projection. Some dupes (like Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man or Lattafa Ana Abiyedh Rouge) outperform their originals.

Are the dupes in this article verified by the fragrance community?

Yes. Every dupe has been cross-checked against multiple community sources including Fragrantica, Parfumo, Basenotes, TikTok and YouTube fragrance reviewers, and Reddit communities. We include honest match percentage estimates and flag known limitations transparently in each card.

Do these dupes last as long as the originals?

Performance varies. Some — like Armaf Club de Nuit Intense Man and Lattafa Bade'e Al Oud — actually outperform their originals in projection and longevity. Others trade some performance for the lower price. We note performance differences in each fragrance card above.

Will the Amazon links work for international shoppers?

All links in this article go to Amazon US (amazon.com). International readers can search the same product names on their local Amazon to find equivalent listings. Lattafa, Armaf, Al Haramain, and Maison Alhambra are widely available globally.

How do you make money from these recommendations?

Decoded Scents earns a small commission when readers purchase through our Amazon Associate links, at no extra cost to the buyer. This funds our research and lets us keep the site free. We only recommend dupes we genuinely verify through community sources — not based on commission rates.
