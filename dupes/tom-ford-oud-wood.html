Seven shared molecules -- oud, rosewood, cardamom, sandalwood, vetiver, amber and musk -- with saffron and vanilla added on top. Those additions are the reason side-by-side reviewers on Basenotes and Fragrantica consistently describe it as smoother, creamier and more refined than Oud Wood rather than merely close to it; a number of them say they prefer it outright. Worth understanding what the 89% means here: two other Oud Wood alternatives in our database score higher and cost less, so this is not the closest match and not the cheapest. It is the one people who own both keep reaching for, at roughly a third of Oud Wood's retail price. If you want the nearest match to the Tom Ford, look at the higher percentages on this page. If you want the better bottle, this is it.

Sources: Basenotes side-by-side reviews, Fragrantica community (owners of both), Jomashop reviews 2024-2026
